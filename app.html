Luggage Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts luggage with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the weight of your bags against a figure you enter, the bags your items take, and the bag fees. It uses no named constants. The bag count, the weight of each bag, the figure to compare against, the items a bag, the travelers, the bags each and the fee are yours to read off your own scale, ticket and booking. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your bags and does not know any airline's rules, your ticket, or whether a bag will be accepted, and it gives no verdict that a bag is allowed, fits or avoids a fee. Every number here is your own entry.

The weight truth

Weight: the bags times the weight of each bag, set against the figure you enter, with the difference.

The pack truth

Pack: the items divided by the items a bag, rounded up, with the empty spaces.

The fee truth

Fee: the travelers times the bags each gives the bags, and the bags times the fee gives the cost.

Luggage Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
